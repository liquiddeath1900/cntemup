How CNTEM'UP Works

Step 1: Set Up Your Phone

Prop your phone so the camera faces a flat surface. You can lean it against a wall, a box, or anything stable.

Step 2: Start the Camera

Open cntemup.com in your browser and tap START. A tripwire line appears on screen.

Step 3: Slide Containers Past the Line

Slide your bottles and cans past the tripwire line one at a time. Each container that crosses gets counted automatically. No tapping, no scanning — just slide and count.

Step 4: See Your Total

Your count and deposit value update in real-time. When you're done, you know exactly how many containers you have and how much your cans are worth.
